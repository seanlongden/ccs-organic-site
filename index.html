FAQ

Answers before you apply.

What does it actually cost?
Discussed on the qualification call. Priced against the deal-size lift we're targeting, not by a fixed number that ignores your context. If we don't think we can 5x the investment inside 180 days, we don't take you on.
What's included, specifically?
All AI agents listed in the "Automations You Get" section above — offer scorer, list builder, cleaner, enrichment scraper, cold-email writer, 28 email frameworks, Lead Formatter prompt writer, bespoke personalisation pipeline, sequencer uploaders, pre-call brief, proposal generator, closing script, engagement agreement generator. Plus weekly coaching with Matt, direct DM access to Sean + Matt, every new agent we ship, and full ownership of every skill forever.
How does the $50k guarantee actually work?
$50,000 in new revenue within 180 days minimum, or you get a full refund. Stipulations you must hit for the guarantee to apply: (1) maintain a 20%+ close rate on qualified calls we book you, (2) average client LTV of $15k+, (3) attend the weekly optimisation call. These aren't hoops — they're the specific things the buyer must do for the mechanism to work. If you do your part and we miss the floor, we refund everything.
What if my cold email is already working?
Then this is even more powerful. If cold email is producing meetings but the meetings are with $400-per-call clients, we're not adding volume — we're changing WHO you're pitching. Same operators, sharper positioning. The volume doesn't change, the deal size does.
What if I've never run cold email?
Then this isn't for you yet. We're built for lead-gen agency owners who already run cold email and are stuck operating it themselves. If you're pre-cold-email, you need a course first — this isn't that.
Do I actually own the AI agents when it's done?
Yes. Every Claude skill, every prompt, every SOP, every proposal template, every agreement template — yours. Copy the folder to your machine. Use them forever, modify them, hand them to a hire, sell your agency with them inside. Not rented. Not licensed. Yours.
Is this done-for-you, coaching, or something else?
Both — that's the point. The AI agents are done-for-you (we build, manage, and evolve them). The repositioning is coaching (weekly work with Matt on your specific context). Neither alone would work. Together they're the offer.
What do the first 30 / 60 / 90 days look like?
Days 0-30: AI agents installed on your infrastructure, offer scored + rewritten, first campaigns spec'd against the reposition. Days 30-60: new campaigns running, weekly coaching, first higher-tier deals in the pipeline. Days 60-90: full agent stack running acquisition and fulfilment, you're touching campaigns only for exceptions, first repositioned deals closing. 90-180 days: pipeline compounds, revenue floor hits, you decide whether to scale headcount or hold.
Can you help me hire an operator or setter?
Yes — with recommendations and screening frameworks. We help you place people, not place them for you. The reason: our members who tried "placed operators" from other programs got junior people who couldn't run the system. Ours works because you own the hiring bar. We help you get it right.
What if I want to pivot niches mid-program?
That's often what the repositioning IS. Every week's coaching includes pipeline review — if the niche isn't converting, we adjust the target market, the offer, or both. Repositioning IS the mechanism.
